This action is permanent and cannot be undone

Delete Account Directly In the App

For immediate, automated account deletion, you can delete your account directly inside the Sooriyan app:

  1. Open the Sooriyan App on your device.
  2. Go to the Profile tab in the navigation menu.
  3. Tap on the Settings gear icon in the top right corner.
  4. Select Account Management.
  5. Tap on Delete Account at the bottom of the screen.
  6. Confirm your choice. Your account and content will be deleted instantly.
