Zero-Tolerance Policy for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Sooriyan, developed by Coderead Academy, maintains a strict zero-tolerance policy toward Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) and Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Sooriyan strictly prohibits users from creating, uploading, publishing, sharing, requesting, promoting, distributing, or otherwise facilitating any content or activity that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

The following activities are strictly prohibited on Sooriyan:

  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form.
  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM), including images, videos, text, illustrations, or other content depicting or facilitating sexual abuse or exploitation of minors.
  • Sexualization of minors.
  • Grooming or attempting to establish inappropriate relationships with children for sexual purposes.
  • Soliciting, requesting, exchanging, or distributing sexual content involving minors.
  • Sextortion, sexual exploitation, trafficking, or any other form of sexual abuse involving children.
  • Any content that promotes, encourages, facilitates, or normalizes the sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
Content Reporting and User Safety

Sooriyan provides users with a mechanism to report inappropriate content, user behavior, or suspected child safety violations.

Users who become aware of content or behavior that may involve CSAE or CSAM should report it immediately through the available reporting or support mechanism within the Sooriyan application or by contacting our Child Safety Point of Contact.

Reports involving child safety are treated as a priority. When we become aware of suspected CSAM or CSAE content, we may take appropriate action, including removing the content, restricting or terminating the responsible account, preserving relevant information where legally appropriate, and reporting the matter to the appropriate authorities as required by applicable law.

Compliance With Applicable Laws

Sooriyan is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations. We cooperate with relevant authorities and take appropriate action when legally required in relation to suspected child sexual abuse, exploitation, or CSAM.

Sooriyan does not knowingly permit the creation, upload, storage, publication, or distribution of content that facilitates the sexual abuse or exploitation of children.

Enforcement

Violations of these Child Safety Standards may result in immediate removal of content, suspension or termination of the user's account, and/or reporting to relevant authorities where required by applicable law.

These Child Safety Standards apply to all users and all content available through Sooriyan.
